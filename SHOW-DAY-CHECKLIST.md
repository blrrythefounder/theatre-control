# Show-Day Checklist

Print this and keep it in the booth.

## Before the house opens

- [ ] Laptop plugged into power (not on battery)
- [ ] Sleep, screen saver and OS notifications **off** (macOS: Focus / Do Not Disturb; Windows: Focus assist)
- [ ] Display set to **Extend**, not Mirror
- [ ] Audio output set to the house sound system, system volume checked
- [ ] Open `index.html` in **Chrome or Edge**
- [ ] **Re-select every video** on the Video Cues tab (amber ⚠ = needs re-selecting)
- [ ] Re-select the **credits music** file
- [ ] Click **Open Projector** → drag to the projector → double-click for fullscreen
- [ ] Press each cue's hotkey once and let it play a few seconds, then `Esc`
- [ ] Check every cue's volume through the house speakers
- [ ] Step through every credits slide on the projector; check spelling
- [ ] Press `B` to confirm blackout works, then `B` again
- [ ] Projector showing black — click back on the **control** window
- [ ] Close every other app and browser tab

## During the show

- Status bar at the top always says what's on the projector.
- **`Esc`** stops all video. **`B`** blacks out everything.
- If you clicked into the projector window by mistake, shortcuts still work — but click back on the control window when you can.
- Don't close or reload the control page while the projector is open.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Pop-up blocked | Click the blocked-pop-up icon in the address bar → *Always allow* → Open Projector again |
| Video won't play | Re-export as **MP4 (H.264 + AAC)** and re-select it |
| No sound from video | Check the cue's volume slider and the laptop's output device |
| Projector window lost behind something | Click **Open Projector** — it brings the existing window to the front |
| Page reloaded by accident | Cues/credits are autosaved; re-select media and reopen the projector |

## After the show

- [ ] Settings → **Save Show File** (backup of cues, hotkeys and credits)
